Which is better?

Lowest listed entry price: JioFiber ₹399 for 30 Mbps versus Airtel ₹499 for 40 Mbps. At 100 Mbps, JioFiber's listed ₹699 is ₹100/month below Airtel's ₹799. This is a tariff comparison, not a guarantee of availability or connection quality.

Before choosing

Check whether wired fiber reaches your building, the actual installation quote, router ownership, service support and any included OTT benefits. Jio's ₹999/150 Mbps plan lists entertainment benefits; bundles vary by plan and tenure.
