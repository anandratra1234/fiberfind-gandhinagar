Best choice by budget

Smallest listed monthly commitment: JioFiber 30 Mbps at ₹399 + GST, subject to installation terms. For a household able to prepay: GTPL's annual 80 or 100 Mbps tariffs may offer lower speed-adjusted cost, but availability and annual lock-in matter.

What should you ask before ordering?

Confirm that fiber reaches your specific address; get the full first-payment amount in writing; check router/installation charges, billing period, refund policy and actual upload speed. A low monthly equivalent is not the same as a month-to-month subscription.
